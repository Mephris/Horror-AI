### Hunter Changelog (Smart Planning & Reactive Patrol)

```
Big pivot in logic today. We initially tried to make the Planner generate a hardcoded list of steps (Route), but realized this turned the AI into a "dumb robot" that stopped reacting to the world while following its list.

We stripped that out and built a "Soft Planning" system.

1.  **"Free Patrol" Mode:** The Planner no longer micro-manages. It now simply sets a context: "FreePatrol" (Stay local, be opportunistic) or "SearchRoom" (Travel far, be systematic).
    * **Benefit:** The Hunter now re-evaluates his options after *every single step*, ensuring he never walks past a new sound or high-priority target just because his "list" told him to go elsewhere.

2.  **"Wall-Hack" Fix (NavMesh Distance):**
    * **Problem:** The Hunter was prioritizing points through walls because we were using `Vector3.Distance` (flying bird distance). He thought a room 2m away through a wall was "close," even though it was a 40m walk down the hall.
    * **Fix:** Implemented `NavMesh.CalculatePath` in the scoring logic. The AI now calculates the *actual walking effort*. If a point is physically close but requires a long walk, the AI correctly identifies it as "Far" and ignores it.

3.  **The "Move-Think-Move" Loop:**
    * Restored the critical loop: Move to best point -> Arrive -> Physical Scan (Glance Perk) -> Move to NEXT best point. 
    * The `ObserveAndScan` node now intelligently switches between "Local Radius Scan" (during Free Patrol) and "Room List Scan" (during Room Search).
```
