#### New Feature: [[VantageSolver (vantage point finder)|The Vantage Solver]]
Implemented Environment Query system style generator of candidate positions in order to get the right vantage point. Is it overkill for an ai that (i do not plan) won't shoot? maybe... or maybe not, dialed this shit down a little bit. 

Right now it generated 3 candidate positions, center and left/right flanks 3 meters away from the target, before filtering it and checking conditions:
- Can i get there? `Navmesh.SamplePosition`
- Is the Line of Sight clear? Can I see it? `Physics.Linecast`
