#### HTN Foundation: The Job System.md

Completed Phase 2 Step 3 of the Stalker Roadmap. Transitioned the AI memory structure from a single variable (currentPatrolTarget) to a dynamic Job Queue. This allows the Hunter to store and execute complex chains of actions (e.g., "Walk here, then Look there, then Wait"), paving the way for the Job Generator.

#### 1. New Data Structure: `HunterJob`

- **Created `HunterJob.cs`**: A non-MonoBehaviour class that acts as a "Task Ticket."
    
- **Job Types:** Defined atomic actions:
    
    - `MoveTo`: Go to a specific Vantage Point (Vector3).
        
    - `Peek`: Stop and look at an Interest Target (Transform).
        
    - `Interact`: Play an animation (Future use).
        
    - `Wait`: Idle for X seconds.
        
- **Separation of Concerns:** The job holds both the **Nav Destination** (Feet) and **Interest Target** (Eyes) separately, supporting the Stalker architecture.
    

#### 2. Brain Upgrade: Job Queue

- **Updated `HunterAI.cs`**:
    
    - Replaced the single-target logic with a `Queue<HunterJob>`.
        
    - Added management methods: `AddJob()`, `GetNextJob()`, `ClearJobs()`.
        
    - **Impact:** The AI can now "Plan Ahead." If we push 3 jobs into the queue, the Behavior Tree will execute them sequentially without needing to stop and re-think between steps.
