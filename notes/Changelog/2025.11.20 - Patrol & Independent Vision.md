### Hunter AI - Smart Patrol & Independent Vision Update

**Summary:**
Moved from a rigid "List-Based Route" system to a fluid "Continuous Chain" system. The Hunter now moves like a predator—constantly adjusting its path without stopping—while its head subconsciously scans the environment for details.

#### 1. Movement: "The Refill Chain"
* **Feature:** Replaced `GetBestPatrolRoute` (Static List) with `GetBestNextPoint` (Dynamic Evaluation).
* **Logic:** The Hunter now maintains a buffer of 2 points (Current & Next). As soon as he gets within **2 meters** of his target, he instantly switches to the Next point and calculates a new Future point.
* **Result:** No more "Stop-Start" robotic braking at every waypoint. Movement is continuous and fluid.
* **Corner Cutting:** Added logic to "skip" the final few meters of a patrol point if the Hunter gets within **5 meters** and can visually confirm it is "Cold" (Empty).

#### 2. Vision: "The Independent Head"
* **Feature:** Decoupled the `FieldOfView` sensor from the main body.
* **Script:** Created `HunterHeadController.cs`.
* **Logic:** While the body follows the NavMesh path, the head independently swivels to look at "Hot" patrol points within a **25m radius**.
* **Optimization:** Implemented Raycasts for vision checks (fast) vs. NavMesh for pathing (accurate).
* **Result:** The Hunter can now "clear" a room by looking into it while walking past in the hallway, without entering.

#### 3. Fixes & Tuning
* **Wall-Hack Fix:** Switched distance calculations from `Vector3.Distance` (Bird) to `NavMesh.CalculatePath` (Walking). The AI no longer prioritizes rooms behind walls.
* **Hierarchy Cleanup:** Structured Hunter prefab with a distinct `Head` child object to support the new multi-FOV setup.
