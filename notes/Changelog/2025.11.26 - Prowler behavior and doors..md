Huge progress on "Game Feel" and Architecture today. We moved away from the robotic "Stop-Start" movement and implemented organic, independent body/head coordination.

Redone behavior tree from evaluation to OnUpdate, OnStart and OnFinish so to make it work like a proper thing... 

The Hunter no longer feels like a NavMeshAgent; he feels like a predator searching for prey.
### 1. Architecture Overhaul: Event-Driven Behavior Tree
We upgraded the core `BehaviorTree.cs` from a stateless `Evaluate()` system to a full Lifecycle system (`OnEnter`, `OnUpdate`, `OnExit`).
* **Why:** Fixed the "Stale Node" bugs where timers/variables wouldn't reset if a behavior was interrupted.
* **Benefit:** We can now interrupt the Hunter mid-action (e.g., mid-peek) and he cleanly resets his state.

### 2. "The Prowler" Movement (Continuous Flow)
* **Refill Chain:** Implemented a buffer system. As the Hunter gets within 2m of a target, he swaps to the next target immediately without braking.
* **The Wiggly Carrot:** Instead of walking in straight lines, the Hunter now pursues a "Ghost Target" 4m ahead on the path that drifts in a Sine Wave pattern. He weaves down hallways naturally.
* **Independent Head:** Decoupled the `FieldOfView` from the body. The `HunterHeadController` now scans for "Hot" points independently while the body walks its own path. Added "Idle Gaze" so he looks around randomly when bored.

### 3. Smart Doorways & "The Creep"
We solved the "Door Hopping" obsession and the "Wall Staring" issues.
* **Smart Prefab:** Created a Door prefab with `DoorConnectionHandler` that auto-wires two patrol points ("In" and "Out") to their respective rooms.
* **The Creep:** When checking a door, the Hunter no longer stops at the frame. He slows to `0.5` speed and drifts 1.5m *into* the room to get a better look.
* **Behavior Cooldowns:** Implemented a "Skill Cooldown" system. Once he peeks a door, he gets "Bored" of doors for 15 seconds, forcing him to enter rooms rather than ping-ponging between doorways.

### 4. The NavMesh Saga (Bug Fixes)
Fixed the critical "Yellow Line" bug where the Hunter would stand still at open doors.
* **Diagnosis:** NavMesh islands (Hall vs Room) were not welded.
* **Fix:** Implemented `NavMeshLink` to bridge the threshold and updated `PerformDoorwayPeek` to iteratively search for valid floor positions (1.5m -> 1.0m -> 0.5m) if the deep target is blocked.

* **Visual Tree:** Updated `HunterAIDebugger` to show proper industry symbols (`[?] Selector`, `[->] Sequence`) and handle stale state visualization (graying out inactive branches).
* **Gizmos:** Added "Dart" visualization to Patrol Points to see exactly where the Hunter will try to Peek.
